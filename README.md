# Cloud Bloom — three flowering gardens

Play: https://andreibohun-hash.github.io/cloud-bloom/

Guide a little rain cloud through three short gardens: Daisy Trail, Tulip Paths,
and Sunflower Circle. Water small plants, grow your cloud, and bloom each garden's
main flower. Larger plants need enough rain coverage.

Dense original 3D foliage bends in the wind and under rainfall. The cloud has an
expressive face, smooth acceleration, and clear level-ups with a larger body,
wider rain circle, more drops, particles, and sound.

Drag or tap to move. Keyboard: WASD / arrows; Escape pauses; R restarts.
All three gardens are available from the selection screen. Completed gardens and
compatible in-progress runs are saved locally in this browser. Existing completed
campaign records are retained; older incompatible partial layouts restart.

Version 5, 20 October 2026. One standalone HTML with embedded fonts and Three.js;
no CDN required. WebAudio sound and reduced-motion support. Three.js is included
under the MIT license; see THREE-LICENSE.
